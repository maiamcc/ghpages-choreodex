Corelli’s Maggot

english Video ↗

Cathy & John Miller (1993)

duple minor longways

introduce gates, out-of-set interaction

Arcangelo Corelli (ish) 2/4 F major BI p. 23 · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

easy

A1

  • (1-4) 1st corners set and turn single (8)
  • (5-8) 1st corners 2-hand turn 1x (8)
  • (all home)

A2

  • (1-8) 2nd corners, the same (16)
  • (all home)

B1

  • (1-4) 1s cross (R sh) and cast down to 2nd place (8)
  • while 2s lead up

B2

  • (1-4) new 2s below, gate the ones down thru middle and back to this place (8)
  • (5-8) familiar 2s above, gate the ones up thru middle and back to this place (8)
  • (new 1st Cs check in)

Teaching Notes

Can teach eye contact moment in the gate.
