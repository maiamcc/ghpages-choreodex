3C

poussettes and R vs. L file practice; dead easy.

9/8 F major BI p. 96 · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

v. easy

A1

  • (1-4) Robins (caller left) CCW lead around standing line (12)
  • (Robin 1 cross above ptnr… hands? skipping?)

A2

  • (1-4) Larks (caller right) CW lead around standing line (12)
  • (Lark 1 cross above ptnr… hands? skipping)

B1

  • Ones “figure 8 poussette”
  • (1-2) (at top) 1s & 2s CW poussette ½ to trade (1st diags fwd) (6)
  • (3-4) (at bottom) 1s & 3s CCW half poussette (1st diags back up) (6)
  • (end: ones at bottom)

B2

  • (1-2) 1s & 3s another half CCW poussette (6)
  • (3-4) 1s & 2s ½ CW poussette (1st diags back up) (6)
  • (end: all home)

C1

  • (1-4) Ones cross & go below (twos move up) (6)
  • (3-4) Ones cross & go below (threes move up) (6)
  • (end: 2, 3, 1)

C2

  • (1-2) Ones lead up to top, cast down to bottom of set (12)
  • (end: 3, 2, 1)

Background

It’s a slipjig!! (dots linked are in G but tune in F)

Teaching Notes

For going around the standing line, may be single file or joined hands, walking or skipping or side-step-skipping. Just be clear!

End of B1 into B2: 1s are doing a full CCW poussette around the 3s, it’s just split over the phrase.
