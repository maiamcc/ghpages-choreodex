Bishop, The

english Video ↗

Thompson (1778) Interp. Pat Shaw (1965)

triple minor longways

easyish triple minor

4/4 BI p. 12 · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

easy

A1

  • (1-2) [caller’s R] 1st lark cast down to 2nd place (2nd lark moving up) (4)
  • (3-4) set to 3rd robin (4)
  • (5-8) 1st lark & 3rd robin 2-hand turn 1x (8)

A2

  • (1-2) [caller’s L] 1st robin cast down to 2nd place (2nd robin moving up) (4)
  • (3-4) set to 3rd lark (4)
  • (5-8) 1st robin & 3rd lark 2-hand turn 1x (8)
  • (ends 2-1-3, proper)

B1

  • (1-4) 2s (above) gate 1s up (8)
  • while 3s at the bottom RSR 1x
  • (5-8) all six circle L ½ (8)

B2

  • (1-4) 3s (above) gate 1s up (8)
  • while 2s at the bottom RSR 1x
  • (5-8) all six circle L ½ (8)

Teaching Notes

Optional simplifications:

  • omit the setting in the As and let that #1 dancer do a lonnng cast down
  • omit the RSRs at bottom during the Bs
