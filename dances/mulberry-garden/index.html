Mulberry Garden, The

english Video ↗

Playford (1670) Interp. Cecil Sharp (1922)

duple minor longways

glossary: up and double and falling back, back to back, bast and lead. BUT orientation might be hard!!

6/4 BI p. 83 · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

v. easy

https://playforddances.com/dances/mulberry-garden/

A1

PART ONE

  • (1-4) all lead up a double and back (8)

A2

  • (1-4) that again (end facing across) (8)

B

  • (1-4) fall back a double away from P, and come forward (8)
  • (5-8) partner 2-hand turn 1x (8)
  • (all home)

A1

PART TWO

  • (1-4) partner back to back (8)

A2

  • (1-4) neighbor back to pack (8)

B

  • (1-2) circle left halfway (4)
  • (3-4) partner 2-hand turn ½ (4)
  • (all in progressed places)
  • (5-8) twos cast down and lead up (8)
  • while ones lead up and cast down
  • (end facing up)

Teaching Notes

1x through dance is 2x through tune.
