Ransom Note

english Video ↗

Susan Kevra (2017)

proper duple longways

glossary: lead, cast, 2HT, fall back, back to back, set

Rachel Bell 6/8 E minor 2008 NIB · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

v. easy

A1

  • (1-4) Ones lead down through twos, cast back to place (8)
  • (5-8) Ones two hand turn 1x (8)

A2

  • (1-4) Twos lead up through the ones, cast back to place (8)
  • (5-8) Twos two hand turn 1x (8)
  • (home)

B1

  • (1-4) Circle left ½, w/ nbr on side fall back (8)
  • (5-8) Ptnr back to back 1x (face nbr) (8)
  • (progressed, crossed over)

B2

  • (1-4) Neighbor back to back 1x (face ptnr) (8)
  • (5-6) Ptnr set R/L (4)
  • (7-8) Ptnr cross RSh (4)
  • (ones stay close ready to lead down)

Background

From Lindsey: “This is the clap-free Joanna B2”.

Original B2 was: N back to back, face P and clap together (self) R (P) together (self) L (P), then swap.
