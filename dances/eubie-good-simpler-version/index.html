Susan Kevra (2024)

proper duple longways

half fig 8s, Rs & Ls, circles and back to backs.

6/8 G major NIB · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

v. easy

A1

  • (1-2) Circle left ½ (4)
  • (3-4) Fall back w/ nbr (4)
  • (5-8) Ptnr back to back 1x (8)
  • (all in diag place)

A2

  • (1-2) Circle left ½ (4)
  • (3-4) Fall back w/ nbr (4)
  • (5-8) Ptnr back to back 1x (8)
  • (all home)

B1

  • (1-4) Ones half fig 8 down (8)
  • (7-8) Twos half fig 8 up (8)
  • (ones above, all xed over)

B2

  • (1-4) Two changes rights & lefts (ptnr RH across, 4steps/pass) (8)
  • (5-8) Ptnr 2HT 1x (8)
  • (uncurl/open up to take hands four with next nbrs)
