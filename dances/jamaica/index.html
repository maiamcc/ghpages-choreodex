Jamaica

english Video ↗

Playford (1670) Interp. Tom Cook (2020)

four couple set (technically 3s and 4s improper, but maybe the teaching note is just “you don’t need to care about YOUR side of the set b/c it changes by your number”.)

some interesting set geography

2/2 F major BI p. 60 · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

v. easy

Introduction

A1

  • (1-4) Up a double and fall back (8)

A2

  • (1-4) Up a double and fall back (8)

B1

  • (1-4) W/ ptnr, set and turn single right (8)
  • (5-8) W/ ptnr, set and turn single right (8)

Figure Part I

A1

  • (1-2) Ends (1s&4s) take RH, take LH on top w/ ptnr (4)
  • (3-4) Ends cross-hand turn halfway (face nbrs up/down) (4)

A2

  • (1-2) Nbrs take RH, take LH on top (4)
  • (3-4) Nbrs cross-hand turn halfway (face across, keep hands) (4)
  • (ends 2-1-4-3)

B1

  • (1-4) W/ nbr, fall back, come forward (8)
  • (5-8) Actives/ends/people now in the middle half figure 8 out thru ends (8)
  • (ends 2-1-4-3, actives opposite home (if we care))
  • (NEW 1st diags (within center 4) look for each other)

Figure Part II

A1

  • (1-4) Actives in center: 1st diags back to back 1x (8)

A2

  • (1-2) Actives in center: 2nd diags back to back 1x (8)

B1

  • (1-4) Actives in center: nbr 2HT 1 ½ (8)
  • (5-8) Ptnr turn ½ & fall back (8)
  • (puts 1s&2s proper/3s&4s imp)
  • (ends 2-4-1-3; new actives on ends get ready)

Background

written as AAB but the B part is twice as long as the A part so one time thru the tune is 32 counts.

Teaching Notes

One time thru the figure is 2x thru the tune.

Run as: intro, 8x figure, intro (18 times thru tune).

Ends = actives.
