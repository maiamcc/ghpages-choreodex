proper duple longways

figures are very simple but the tune is FUNKY. Maybe good to practice counting weird time sigs?

3/2 A major BI p. 65 · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

easy

A1

  • (1-2) Ones cast down, twos lead up (6)
  • (3-4) Ones back to back 1x (6)

A2

  • (1-2) Ones cast up, twos lead down (6)
  • (3-4) Ones back to back 1x (6)
  • (home - all face out, join hands with nbr)

B1

  • (1-2) W/ nbr, lead out 3 steps, turn to face nbr, lead back three steps (6)
  • (3-4) Ptnr cross R Sh, R Sh loop to face in (6)

B2

  • (1-2) 2nd diags trade R Sh, 1st diags trade R Sh (6)
  • (3-4) Ptnr 2HT 1x (6)
  • (ones ready to cast)
