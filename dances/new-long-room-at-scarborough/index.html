New Long Room at Scarborough

english Video ↗

Johnson (1748)

proper duple longways

slipjig, casts and leads and sets plus Rs and Ls

9/8 G major NIB · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

v. easy

A1

  • (1-2) Ones cast down (twos lead up) (6)
  • (3-4) Ones 2HT 1x (6)

A2

  • (1-2) Twos cast down (ones lead up) (6)
  • (3-4) Twos 2HT 1x (6)
  • (home)

B1

  • (1-2) 1st diags set (triple time) (6)
  • (3-4) 1st diags 2HT 1x (6)

B2

  • (1-2) 2nd diags set (triple time) (6)
  • (3-4) 2nd diags 2HT 1x (6)
  • (still home)

C1

  • (1-2) Ones lead down two steps, turn alone & lead back (6)
  • (3-4) Ones cast down one place (twos lead up) (6)

C2

  • (1-4) Four changes Rs & Ls (pntr RH across, 3 steps/change (12)
  • (ones ready to cast)

Background

it’s a slipjig!
