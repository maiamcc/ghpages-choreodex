Highland Farewell

english Video ↗

Pat Petersen (2013)

proper duple longways

half fig-8, Rs & Ls

4/4 A minor NIB · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

v. easy

A1

  • (1-4) Ptnrs RH turn 1x (8)
  • (5-8) Circle left (8)

A2

  • (1-4) Circle right (8)
  • (5-8) Ptnrs LH turn 1x (8)
  • (all home)

B1

  • (1-4) Ones pull by left to cross, cast down (twos move up) (8)
  • (5-8) Ones ½ fig 8 up (8)
  • (all progressed)

B2

  • (1-8) Four changes Rs & Ls (ptnr RH across, 4steps/pass) (16)
  • (acknowledge next nbrs)

Teaching Notes

Someone will have an awk transition into the A1 circle
