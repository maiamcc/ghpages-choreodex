Midnight Ramble

english Video ↗

Thompson (1773) Interp. George Fogg

proper duple longways

½ fig 8 and Rs/Ls; clapping adds a little zing to glossary stuff

2/2 D major BI p. 75 · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

v. easy

A1

  • (1-4) Ones clap WHILE cross RSh, go below one place (twos move up) (8)
  • (5-8) Ones two hand turn 1 ½ (8)

A2

  • (1-4) Twos clap WHILE cross RSh, go below one place (ones move up) (8)
  • (5-8) Twos two hand turn 1 ½ (8)
  • (all home)

B1

  • (1-4) Ones cross (no clap), go below (twos move up) (8)
  • (5-8) Ones ½ fig 8 above twos (8)
  • (ones below, all proper)

B2

  • (1-8) Four changes rights & lefts (ptnr R across, 4steps/change) (16)
  • (ones ID next twos)
