Mile Of Smiles

english

Joseph Pimentel (2012)

duple minor longways

standard A part with a chase in the B, still mostly glossary moves.

Dave Wiesler 4/4 C major 2011 BIII p. 94 · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

easy

A1

  • (1-4) 1st corners set and turn single (8)
  • (5-8) 1st corners 2-hand turn 1x (8)
  • (all home)

A2

  • (1-8) 2nd corners, the same (16)
  • (all home)

B1

  • (1-4) [caller’s L] 1st robin cast down into second place and across the set, P following (8)
  • while 2s move up
  • (5-8) Neighbors R-hand turn 1x (8)

B2

  • (1-4) [caller’s L] 2nd robin cast down into second place and across the set, P following (8)
  • while 1s move up
  • (unprogressed places, opposite home side)
  • (5-6) All R-hand star halfway (4)
  • (7-8) Turn single left (4)
  • (new 1st Cs, acknowledge)

Background

Pimentel:

For Dave Wiesler, an awesome pianist and tunesmith. Dave wrote the tune and dedicated it to his mother, Helen Wiesler, who grew up on a farm in southern Indiana called 'Mile of Smiles.'

Teaching Notes

After the N R-hand turn, the 2s are already both facing the correct direction out of the set (caller’s L).

Original choreo indicates that during the chase figure, inactive couples only move up after 4 counts of waiting, but in practice I don’t think anyone does this.

Technically the chase figure lands the couple that did it in “improper”.
