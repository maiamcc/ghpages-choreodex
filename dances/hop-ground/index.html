The Hop Ground

english Video ↗

Preston (1794) Interp. Bert Simons (1991)

proper duple longways

a slightly different take on setting (2 v 1); Rs & Ls.

6/8 D major BI p. 53 · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

v. easy

A1

  • (1-4) Ones (with joined hands) & their 1st diag nbr, set twice (8)
  • (5-8) These three circle left 1x (8)
  • (all home)

A2

  • (1-4) Ones (with joined hands) & their 2st diag nbr, set twice (8)
  • (5-8) These three circle left 1x (8)
  • (all home)

B1

  • (1-4) Ones cross, go below (twos move up) (8)
  • (5-8) Ones 2HT 1 ½ (8)
  • (all progressed)

B2

  • (1-4) Four changes Rs & Ls (ptnr RH across, 4steps/change) (16)
  • (acknowledge next nbrs)
