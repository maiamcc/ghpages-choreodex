Kalia Kilban (2008)

improper duple longways

waltz time, easy figures but a little piece-y

Ye Banks and Braes Traditional 3/4 C major BIII p. 107 · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

easy

A1

  • (1-2) Ones lead down center (6)
  • (3-4) Ones CA twirl (6)
  • (5-6) Ones lead back up to place (6)
  • (7-8) Ones 2HT ½ home (6)

A2

  • (1-2) Twos lead up center (6)
  • (3-4) Twos CA twirl (6)
  • (5-6) Twos lead back down to place (6)
  • (7-8) Ptnr 2HT ½, open to face nbrs (6)

B1

  • (1-2) Circle left ½ (6)
  • (3-6) Ptnr CW half poussette (2nd diags/larks backing) (12)
  • (7-8) Ptnr 2HT ½, open to face nbrs, keep mirror hands (6)
  • (all home)

B2

  • (1-2) Set facing nbrs (6)
  • (3-4) Nbrs pass R Sh up/down (6)
  • (5-8) Partner two-hand turn 1x (12)
  • (ones open to face down, keep mirror hands to lead down)

Background

joined setting could be interesting to practice.

Note the asymmetrical phrasing in B1!
