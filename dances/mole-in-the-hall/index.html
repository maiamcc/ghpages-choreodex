the glossary-est dance that ever glossaried. Cast, lead, turn single, set, 2ht

Mairi’s Wedding?? idk trad. 4/4 G major · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

v. easy

A1

  • (1-4) Ones lead down between twos, cast up outside to place (8)
  • (5-8) Ones set to P, turn single R (8)

A1

  • (1-4) Twos lead up between ones, cast down outside to place (8)
  • (5-8) Twos set to P, turn single R (8)
  • (all are home)

B1

  • (1-4) 1st corners set and change by R shoulder (8)
  • (1-4) 2nd corners set and change by R shoulder (8)
  • (1s below 2s, all opposite home)

B2

  • (1-4) Circle left 1x (8)
  • (5-8) 2-hand turn 1 & ½ to change places, ending on home side (8)
  • (1s end close, ready to lead down through new 2s)

Background

The tune choice doesn’t really matter?? Any generic marchy 64-count AABB will do!

Teaching Notes

Optionally, setting in A1/A2 may be for everyone (i.e. “all set to partner, turn single R”) instead of just the active couples.
