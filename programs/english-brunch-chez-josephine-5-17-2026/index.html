The Farmer’s Joy

english

Joseph Pimentel (2012)

duple minor longways

sorta kinda half fig-8 practice, mostly just delightful

Adam Broome 6/8 A major 2004 BIII p. 56 · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

v. easy

A1

  • (1-4) 1st corners set and turn single (8)
  • (5-8) 1st corners 2-Hand turn (8)

A2

  • (1-4) 2nd corners set and turn single (8)
  • (5-8) 2nd corners 2-Hand turn (8)

B1

  • (1-4) 1s lead down the center, turn as couple (wheel CW) (8)
  • (5-8) lead back, cross and cast to 2nd place* (2s moving up) (8)

B2

  • (1-4) RH star (8)
  • (5-8) LH star (8)
  • (look for new Ns; new 1st Cs, acknowledge)

Teaching Notes

*in practice, this turns into half-figure 8

Pimentel:

This is a simple dance which works well for new dancers, and the tune is so wonderfully joyful that everyone seems to enjoy it.

B1. I like each figure to fit two bar phrases, and for all four figures to feel like one fluid movement. Though I do not often use the words “gate” and “half figure eight,” I think newer dancers begin to get the feel for those figures here without even knowing it. That is intentional.

B2. Encourage intermediate dancers to start the star L on their right foot, so the new first corners have a nice transition into the setting at the top of

Rufty Tufty

english

Playford (1651) Interp. Cecil Sharp (1911)

2-couple

dead simple, kind of boring, also somehow harder than it should be

2/2 D major BI p. 111 · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

v. easy

I) A1

  • (1-4) W/ P, forward a double (towards Ns) and back (8)

I) A2

  • (1-4) Repeat (8)

B1

  • (1-4) Face P, set and turn single R (8)

B2

  • (1-4) Face P, set and turn single R (8)

C1

  • (1-2) (Facing away from Ns) w/ P, out a double (4)
  • (3-4) face in, back a double (4)
  • (5-6) turn single (away from P), take hands with N (4)

C2

  • (1-2) (Facing away from Ps) w/ N, out a double (4)
  • (3-4) face in, back a double (4)
  • (5-6) turn single (away from N) (4)

II) A1 & A2:

  • side right and left with P (8), repeat

III) A1 & A2:

  • arm right and left with P (8), repeat

Michael and All Angels

english

Fried de Metz Herman

duple minor longways

Air Henry Purcell 6/8 A minor 1690 BI p. 75 · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

hard

A1

  • (1-2) 1st corners R-shoulder round 1x (6)
  • (3-4) cast L around N to C’s place (6)
  • (5-7) L-hand turn 1x, ending close (9)
  • (8) fall back into C’s place (3)

A2

  • (1-2) 2nd corners L-shoulder round 1x (6)
  • (3-4) cast R around P to C’s place (6)
  • (5-7) R-hand turn 1x, ending close (9)
  • (8) fall back into C’s place (3)
  • (Everyone has swapped with corner, 1s below 2s)

B

  • (1-2) RH star ½ (6)
  • (3-4) 1st C’s change places (passing face to face w/ 2 slow chasse steps*, changing hands after first step) (6)
  • (5-6) LH star ½ (6)
  • (7-8) all turn single R (expansive) (6)

C

  • (1-3) single file circle CW ¾ to progressed places (12)
  • (4-6) w/ P, poussette ½ (continuing CW) (12)

D

  • (1-2) (keeping hands w/ P) balance fwd & back (6)
  • (3-4) 1s cast down & 2s lead up (6)

Teaching Notes

*shasay steps: step-together (to the right)

All casts in A are around the person on your own side (in A1, 1st Cs cast around N; in A2, 2nd Cs cast around P)

Prince William

english

Walsh (1731) Interp. Pat Shaw (1960)

3-couple longways

2/2 A major BI p. 100 · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

hard

A1

  • (1-8) Crossover mirror hey: 1s cross down, 2s up the outside, 3s meet & lead up (16)

A2

  • (1-8) That again, 1s crossing back to own side (all home) (16)

B1

  • (1-4) 1s cross & go below to middle place while 2s lead up (8)
  • (5-8) then 1s 2-hand turn 1 & 1⁄2 to end original side (8)*
  • *locate corners now!!

B2

  • (1-8) Repeat B1 with 2s active (/1s leading up); all are home

A3

  • 1s turn contra corners:
  • (1-4) (2s move up outside to 1st place) 1s turn first corner by R (8)
  • (5-8) 1s (in middle) turn P by L (8)

A4

  • (1-4) 1s turn second corner by R (8)
  • (5-8) 1s (in middle) turn P by L (8) and face R file, keeping LH

B3

  • 1s solo track:
  • (1-4) 1s lead through the two R-file dancers and cast back to middle place (8)
  • (5-8) 2-hand turn 1 & ½ (8), end facing L file

B4

  • (1-4) 1s lead through the two L-file dancers and cast back to middle place
  • (5-8) 2-hand turn 1 & ½ (8), moving down to 3rd place WHILE
  • @ end of phrase, 3s cast up to 2nd place (4), staying wide & facing up for mirror hey
  • ends 2-3-1

Teaching Notes

  • Progression: 2-3-1
  • This dance is double length. Make sure the musicians know the tune is played two full times.
  • the simple mirror hey would be: wide in the center and tight on the ends (starts 1s and 3s close facing into the set, 2s wide facing up). The crossover addition is that at the top of the phrase, the 1s switch places. Note that they are still going BETWEEN the twos as they cross (resist the urge to cross wide)
  • identify corners at the end of B1. These are the corners for CC, but the approach is funky.
  • starting CC in A3:
    • L-file 1 is going all the way to the bottom to turn their opposite #3.
    • 2s move up to 1st place AS R-file #1 approaches their opposite #2; by the time the allemande actually happens, the 2s are in 1st place.
    • By A4, you’re turning the rest of CC from the expected position.
    • (To find initial corners, L-file #1 crosses in front of P, as they have further to go.)

Softly Good Tummas

english

Nathaniel Kynaston (1718) Interp. Andrew Shaw (2002)

duple minor longways

4/4 D minor BII p. 120 · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

mod.

A1

  • (1-4) Single file circle left halfway (R sh. to center) (8)
  • (5-6) Face center, walk forward to meet (4)
  • (7-8) Turn single L and clap on the last beat (4)

A2

  • (1-4) Single file circle right halfway (L sh. to center) (8)
  • (5-6) Face center, walk forward to meet (4)
  • (7-8) Turn single R and clap on the last beat (4)

B1

  • (1-2) Ones quick cast while twos lead up
  • (3-4) Set to partner across
  • (5-8) Two changes of rights and lefts with hands
  • End close

B2

  • (1-2) Fall back setting (4)
  • (3-4) Cross, passing partner by right shoulder, face in (4)
  • (5-8) 1s right hand turn while moving down into 2nd place
  • while twos cast up the outside into 1st place

Teaching Notes

I always forget what shoulder goes to the center in A2, but it’s the one you turned over at the end of A1! (Maybe worth noting; whatever shoulder was pointed in for the circle, you turn over the opposite one.)

Sapphire Sea

english

Christine Robb (2015)

duple minor longways

a fave! Dolphin hey.

Tom Kruskal’s Emily Troll & Amelia Mason 2/2 F major (orig. A major) BIII p. 121

Choreography

mod.

A

  • (1-4) Circle left 1x (8)
  • (5-8) 1st corners turn by R 1x (8)
  • (9-12) 2nd corners turn by L 1x (8)
  • (all are home)
  • (13-16) 1s cast down and come up the middle (8)
  • while 2s lead up and cast onto the end of a line of 4
  • ending facing the R side of the hall, except the R file dancer who faces in

B

  • (1-8) Dolphin hey (1s as a unit pass R file dancer by right to start) (16)
  • (ending in a line of four facing up, 1s in the middle)
  • (9-12) Up a double, fall back (8)
  • (13-16) Gate (2s gate 1s up and through the middle) (8)
  • (ending progressed: release early to take hands with next)

Teaching Notes

Robb (I think):

Tempo is flexible. Choose whatever makes you happy (I personally prefer it around 112bpm - a little on the faster side than slower side, but basically pretty middle-of-the-road).

This is not a crowded hall dance – it wants lots of space.

The circle and turns in A move quickly – the rest is more laid-back

Barbarini’s Tambourine

english

Walsh (1745) Interp. Jacqueline Schwab (1987)

duple minor longways

2/4 D major BI p. 8 · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

mod.

A1

  • (1-8) 1st corners cast left around neighbor, cross through the middle passing corner by left shoulder, and loop right around partner to end in corner’s place (16)
  • (1st corners are swapped)

A2

  • (1-8) 2nd corners, similarly, cast right around partner, cross through the middle passing corner by right shoulder, and loop left around partner to end in corner’s place (16)
  • (All are halfway around)

B1

  • (1-2) All fall back with neighbor (4)
  • (3-4) Cross, passing partner by right shoulder (4)
  • (5-8) Neighbors back to back along (8)
  • (9-12) Partners back to back across (8)

B2

  • (1-8) Four changes of rights and lefts (16)
  • (9-12) Partners two hand turn (8)

Teaching Notes

Hardest part is remembering what shoulder to cast / pass with. Casts are always up/down the outside; whichever shoulder was near your N, that’s the one you pass P by

Also, note asymmetrical tune phrasing: As are 16 counts, Bs are 24 counts.

Candles in the Dark

english Video ↗

Loretta Holz (2006)

duple minor longways

a fave; assisted figure-8s.

Jonathan Jensen 3/4 B minor Barnes II

Choreography

easy

A1

  • Assisted half figure-8s
  • (1-4) 1s assisted half figure-8 down through 2s (12)
  • (5-8) [caller’s L] these dancers do half figure-8 thru other two (12)

A2

  • Assisted half figure-8s, con’t.
  • (1-4) [caller’s R] these dancers do half figure-8 thru other two (12)
  • (5-8) 2s (now above) assisted half figure-8 down through 1s, end close (12)
  • (all are progressed, improper)

B1

  • (1-4) mirror back to back w/ Ns (2s in center down thru the 1s) (12)
  • (5-8) circle L all the way (12)

B2

  • (1-4) mirror back to back w/ Ns (1s in center up thru the 2s) (12)
  • (5-8) P RSR 1 ½ to end proper (12)

Teaching Notes

Asst. half fig-8: two dancers take RH in RH, dancer on the R gently guides the LH dancer to go first in their fig-8 path.
