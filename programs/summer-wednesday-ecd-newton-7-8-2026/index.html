Knole Park

english Video ↗

Voight (1809) Interp. A. Simons (1961)

duple minor longways

corner geography?? but piece-y, odd momentum changes.

Bishop 2/4 D major 1788 BI p. 65 · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

v. easy

A1

  • (1-4) Circle left (8)
  • (5-6) 1st corners change by R sh (4)
  • (7-8) 2nd corners change by R sh (4)
  • (All halfway around)

A2

  • (1-8) Repeat A1 (16)
  • (All home)

B1

  • (1-4) Ones lead down the middle, turn alone (leisurely) (8)
  • (5-8) Return (zippy, opt. skipping), 1s cast down while 2s move up (8)
  • (All progressed)

B2

  • (1-8) w/ P, full poussette CCW (2nd diagonal (original 1st corners) moving forward) (16)

Teaching Notes

B2. Poussette is CCW, with current first diagonal dancers backing up to start. Another angle on it; current 2nd diags. go forward first, these are the dancers who started as 1st Cs; so if you went first in the A part trades, you go forward in the poussette.

The Minor Spaniard

english

Jenny Beer (2000)

duple minor longways

dead simple moves with interesting recombinations

6/8 E minor BII p. 85 · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

v. easy

A1

  • (1-4) 1st corners set and turn single R (8)
  • (5-8) 1L “defect” and two hand turn partner (8)

A2

  • (1-4) 2nd corners set and turn single R (8)
  • (5-8) 2L “defect” and two hand turn partner (8)

B1

  • (1-2) 1s lead down the middle (4)
  • (3-4) 1s face each other and set (4)
  • (5-8) 1s lead up and cast off while 2s lead up (8)
  • (All progressed)

B2

  • (1-4) Circle L (8)
  • (5-8) 2s gate 1s up through the middle and around to 2nd place (8)

Background

Beer:

Background: Designed as a less strenuous version of “The Spaniard,” from Thompson, 1777.

Tune: The Minor Spaniard, by Jenny Beer. A minor key variation on “The Spaniard” tune.

Peridot Ring

english

Susan Kevra (2017)

3-couple longways

practice swirly siding, meanwhiles, your position in a 3C.

Rachel Bell 2/2 C major 2017 BIII p. 109

Choreography

v. easy

A1

  • (1-4) Taking inside hands, partners lead up a double and back, turning in toward partner to face down (8)
  • (5-8) Lead down a double and fall back (8)

A2

  • (1-4) Partners swirly siding (over by L, back by R) (8)
  • (5-8) All set to P and turn single R (8)

B1

  • (1-4) Hands 6 circle L ½, drop hands and face partner (8)
  • (5-8) (w/o hands partner) balance F&B, cross by R, turn over R to face partner (8)
  • (3-2-1 proper)

B2

  • (1-4) Top couple (3s) cross by R, go below to middle place (8)
  • while middle couple lead up
  • while bottom couple back to back
  • (5-8) Partners 2-hand turn (middles 1 ½, others 1x)
  • (Ending 2-3-1)

Teaching Notes

For a 2C set: in B1, circle 1x; in B2, everyone two hand turn 1x

For a 4C set: 4s don’t dance B1 (only top three circle and cross); instead of bottoms back to back, original 1s (now in 3rd place) cast down and 4s lead up. (Could have them cross and cast, then turn 1.5x, so it’s symmetrical??)

Sunlight Through Draperies

english Video ↗

Tom Roby (2004)

proper duple longways

simple figures but some interesting recombinations, could be good practice for piece-y/odd phrasing.

Judy and Jim’s Wedding Larry Unger 3/4 D major NIB · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

easy

A1

  • (1-3) 1st diags ¾ back to back (R sh) (until L Sh to L Sh) (9)
  • (4) 1st diags R Sh turn single to place (3)
  • (5-8) Ptnr R sh ‘round 1x (12)

A2

  • (1-3) 2nd diags ¾ L Sh back to back (until R Sh to R Sh) (9)
  • (4) 2nd diags L Sh turn single to place (3)
  • (5-8) Ptnr LEFT sh ‘round 1x (12)
  • (all home)

B1

  • (1-4) Circle RIGHT 1x (12)
  • (5-8) Balance the ring, turn single R Sh (12)

B2

  • (1-4) Circle left 1x (12)
  • (5-6) Balance the ring (ones just balance in and not back out?) (6)
  • (7-8) Ones cast down WHILE twos lead up (6)
  • (new first diags acknowledge)

Teaching Notes

Note slightly atypical phrasing in A; may be funky for noodling, check in with musicians.

Childgrove

english

Playford (1701) Interp. Cecil Sharp (1922)

duple minor longways (originally published improper)

siding, full fig 8. Different speeds of 2HT.

2/2 D minor BI p. 20 · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

easy

A1

  • (1-4) Partners side across (L sh, R sh, swirly) (8)
  • (5-8) Partners back to back (8) (face N on side)

A2

  • (1-8) Repeat A1 with neighbor along (16)

B1

  • (1-4) Neighbors two hand turn 1 ½ (opt: skipping), open up across (8)
  • (5-8) Partners two hand turn (8)
  • (All progressed)

B2

  • (1-8) Ones figure eight up through the twos (opt.: skipping) (16)

Teaching Notes

As: Transition from siding into back-to-back is very slick; from back-to-back P -> siding N by L has a momentum change.

B1: Can explicitly note that the two hand turns have different speeds or let dancers figure it out.

B2: Can be a double figure eight for more experienced dancers.
