Indian Queen (MM Var.)

english Video ↗

Playford (1701) Interp. Cecil Sharp (1922)

duple minor longways

simple glossary; corners, stars, back to back etc.

2/2 D major BI p. 57 · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

v. easy

A1

  • (1-4) 1st corners set, turn single (8)
  • (5-8) 1st corners two hand turn (8)
  • (all home)

A2

  • (1-8) 2nd corners, the same (16)
  • (all home)

B1

  • (1-4) Star R (8)
  • (5-8) Star L (8)
  • (all home)

B2

  • (1-4) Partners back to back (8)
  • (all home)
  • (5-8) 1s leisurely cast down to second place (8)
  • while 2s lead up (after 4 beats)
  • (new first Cs, acknowledge)

Background

An even easier variation of Heather Webster’s easy B2 modification.

(The original dance B2 has P back to back, and then three changes of a circular hey (PR, NL, PR), on last pass turning back in to face across.)

Teaching Notes

There’s a lottt of time for the cast/lead at the end of B2; consider a beat or two of eye contact for the 1s before they peel back to cast. 2s can wait four counts before leading up.

Peridot Ring

english

Susan Kevra (2017)

3-couple longways

practice swirly siding, meanwhiles, your position in a 3C.

Rachel Bell 2/2 C major 2017 BIII p. 109

Choreography

v. easy

A1

  • (1-4) Taking inside hands, partners lead up a double and back, turning in toward partner to face down (8)
  • (5-8) Lead down a double and fall back (8)

A2

  • (1-4) Partners swirly siding (over by L, back by R) (8)
  • (5-8) All set to P and turn single R (8)

B1

  • (1-4) Hands 6 circle L ½, drop hands and face partner (8)
  • (5-8) (w/o hands partner) balance F&B, cross by R, turn over R to face partner (8)
  • (3-2-1 proper)

B2

  • (1-4) Top couple (3s) cross by R, go below to middle place (8)
  • while middle couple lead up
  • while bottom couple back to back
  • (5-8) Partners 2-hand turn (middles 1 ½, others 1x)
  • (Ending 2-3-1)

Teaching Notes

For a 2C set: in B1, circle 1x; in B2, everyone two hand turn 1x

For a 4C set: 4s don’t dance B1 (only top three circle and cross); instead of bottoms back to back, original 1s (now in 3rd place) cast down and 4s lead up. (Could have them cross and cast, then turn 1.5x, so it’s symmetrical??)

Key To The Cellar

english

Jenny Beer (2004)

triple minor longways

tricky bit is the heys for 3

Traditional 3/2 E minor BII p. 40 · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

easy

A1

  • (1-2) 1s cast down to middle place while twos move up (6)
  • (3-4) 3s gate the 1s down and around (6)

A2

  • (1-2) Lines at the side, forward and back (6)
  • (3-4) 2s gate the 1s up and around (6)
  • 1s face neighbors diagonally to the R

B1

  • (1-4) Heys for 3 across the dance (12)
  • 1s initiate by passing right diagonal dancer by the left shoulder (splitting the couple)

B2

  • (1-2) 1s RSR (6)
  • (3-4) All two hand turn partner (6)

Background

Beer:

“Key to the Cellar” is an old name for this tune which has many variants, names, meters, and lyrics. It’s better known today as Cam’ Ye O’er Frae France, after the political lyrics Scots put to the tune in 1715.

Teaching Notes

Keep the heys tight—there are only 12 steps to complete them.

Childgrove

english

Playford (1701) Interp. Cecil Sharp (1922)

duple minor longways (originally published improper)

siding, full fig 8. Different speeds of 2HT.

2/2 D minor BI p. 20 · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

easy

A1

  • (1-4) Partners side across (L sh, R sh, swirly) (8)
  • (5-8) Partners back to back (8) (face N on side)

A2

  • (1-8) Repeat A1 with neighbor along (16)

B1

  • (1-4) Neighbors two hand turn 1 ½ (opt: skipping), open up across (8)
  • (5-8) Partners two hand turn (8)
  • (All progressed)

B2

  • (1-8) Ones figure eight up through the twos (opt.: skipping) (16)

Teaching Notes

As: Transition from siding into back-to-back is very slick; from back-to-back P -> siding N by L has a momentum change.

B1: Can explicitly note that the two hand turns have different speeds or let dancers figure it out.

B2: Can be a double figure eight for more experienced dancers.

Barbarini’s Tambourine

english

Walsh (1745) Interp. Jacqueline Schwab (1987)

duple minor longways

2/4 D major BI p. 8 · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

mod.

A1

  • (1-8) 1st corners cast left around neighbor, cross through the middle passing corner by left shoulder, and loop right around partner to end in corner’s place (16)
  • (1st corners are swapped)

A2

  • (1-8) 2nd corners, similarly, cast right around partner, cross through the middle passing corner by right shoulder, and loop left around partner to end in corner’s place (16)
  • (All are halfway around)

B1

  • (1-2) All fall back with neighbor (4)
  • (3-4) Cross, passing partner by right shoulder (4)
  • (5-8) Neighbors back to back along (8)
  • (9-12) Partners back to back across (8)

B2

  • (1-8) Four changes of rights and lefts (16)
  • (9-12) Partners two hand turn (8)

Teaching Notes

Hardest part is remembering what shoulder to cast / pass with. Casts are always up/down the outside; whichever shoulder was near your N, that’s the one you pass P by

Also, note asymmetrical tune phrasing: As are 16 counts, Bs are 24 counts.

Old Wife Behind the Fire

english

Neal (1726) Interp. Fogg/Jackson (1990)

duple minor longways

glossary (set, cast, lines of 4, Rs and Ls)

4/4 G mixolydian BII p. 97 · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

v. easy

A1

  • (1-4) 1s set, cast down while 2s lead up (8)
  • (5-8) 1s two hand turn (8)

A2

  • (1-4) 2s set, cast down while 1s lead up (8)
  • (5-8) 2s two hand turn (8)
  • (All home)

B1

  • (1-4) 1s cross, go below while 2s move up (8)
  • (5-8) 2s cross, go below, come up the center
  • while 1s move up and cast onto ends of a line of 4 (8)

B2

  • (1-4) Up a double, fall back, bend the line (8)
  • (5-8) Two changes rights and lefts (PR, NL) (8)

Faithless Nancy Dawson

english Video ↗

Anna Bidder (1937/1964)

duple minor longways

circular hey practice? Maybe a smidge fast.

A-Rovin’ Traditional 4/4 D major BI p. 38

Choreography

v. easy

A

  • (1-4) 1s lead down, cast up to place (8)
  • (5-8) 2s lead up, cast down to place (8)
  • (9-12) Partners back to back (8)

B

  • (1-2) Set, falling back (4)
  • (3-4) Turn single forwards (4)
  • (5-8) Three changes of a circular hey (PR, NL, PR) (8)

Background

Some debate about the composition date of this dance: there’s a reference to three unnamed dances by Anna Bidder in a 1937 publication of English Dance & Song, but the dance itself was not published until 1964.

Teaching Notes

B. Prefer turn single right here

Softly Good Tummas

english

Nathaniel Kynaston (1718) Interp. Andrew Shaw (2002)

duple minor longways

4/4 D minor BII p. 120 · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

mod.

A1

  • (1-4) Single file circle left halfway (R sh. to center) (8)
  • (5-6) Face center, walk forward to meet (4)
  • (7-8) Turn single L and clap on the last beat (4)

A2

  • (1-4) Single file circle right halfway (L sh. to center) (8)
  • (5-6) Face center, walk forward to meet (4)
  • (7-8) Turn single R and clap on the last beat (4)

B1

  • (1-2) Ones quick cast while twos lead up
  • (3-4) Set to partner across
  • (5-8) Two changes of rights and lefts with hands
  • End close

B2

  • (1-2) Fall back setting (4)
  • (3-4) Cross, passing partner by right shoulder, face in (4)
  • (5-8) 1s right hand turn while moving down into 2nd place
  • while twos cast up the outside into 1st place

Teaching Notes

I always forget what shoulder goes to the center in A2, but it’s the one you turned over at the end of A1! (Maybe worth noting; whatever shoulder was pointed in for the circle, you turn over the opposite one.)

Knole Park

english Video ↗

Voight (1809) Interp. A. Simons (1961)

duple minor longways

corner geography?? but piece-y, odd momentum changes.

Bishop 2/4 D major 1788 BI p. 65 · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

v. easy

A1

  • (1-4) Circle left (8)
  • (5-6) 1st corners change by R sh (4)
  • (7-8) 2nd corners change by R sh (4)
  • (All halfway around)

A2

  • (1-8) Repeat A1 (16)
  • (All home)

B1

  • (1-4) Ones lead down the middle, turn alone (leisurely) (8)
  • (5-8) Return (zippy, opt. skipping), 1s cast down while 2s move up (8)
  • (All progressed)

B2

  • (1-8) w/ P, full poussette CCW (2nd diagonal (original 1st corners) moving forward) (16)

Teaching Notes

B2. Poussette is CCW, with current first diagonal dancers backing up to start. Another angle on it; current 2nd diags. go forward first, these are the dancers who started as 1st Cs; so if you went first in the A part trades, you go forward in the poussette.

The Farmer’s Joy

english

Joseph Pimentel (2012)

duple minor longways

sorta kinda half fig-8 practice, mostly just delightful

Adam Broome 6/8 A major 2004 BIII p. 56 · Sheet music ↗

Choreography

v. easy

A1

  • (1-4) 1st corners set and turn single (8)
  • (5-8) 1st corners 2-Hand turn (8)

A2

  • (1-4) 2nd corners set and turn single (8)
  • (5-8) 2nd corners 2-Hand turn (8)

B1

  • (1-4) 1s lead down the center, turn as couple (wheel CW) (8)
  • (5-8) lead back, cross and cast to 2nd place* (2s moving up) (8)

B2

  • (1-4) RH star (8)
  • (5-8) LH star (8)
  • (look for new Ns; new 1st Cs, acknowledge)

Teaching Notes

*in practice, this turns into half-figure 8

Pimentel:

This is a simple dance which works well for new dancers, and the tune is so wonderfully joyful that everyone seems to enjoy it.
